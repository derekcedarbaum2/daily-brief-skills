# Setup interview coverage

Use these areas as a coverage map, not a questionnaire to read aloud.
Start with existing answers and ask only one unresolved question per turn.
Let the user choose quick setup and return to optional details later.

| Area | Facts to establish | Optional follow-up areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and scope | Preferred name or alias, timezone, work/personal/household scope | Travel timezones, print preferences |
| Goals | Main responsibilities, ranked outcomes for the next four to six weeks | Projects to defer or stop |
| Work | Office and remote days, work windows, recurring meetings, commute each way | Shift work, variable schedules, location exceptions |
| Daily rhythm | Wake/sleep flexibility, energy windows, protected time | Partner time, focus habits, known distractions |
| Household | People or roles whose schedules affect the user | Dependent care, pets, recurring appointments |
| Shared care | Standing days, alternating patterns, start/end boundaries, owners | Anchor date, school-closure and holiday exceptions |
| School or daycare | Days, arrival/pickup windows, travel and prep | Official calendar, early releases, closure rules |
| Handoffs | Drop-off and pickup owner by day, latest departure | Backup owner, confirmation and travel conflicts |
| Activities | Recurrence, arrival/start/end, travel, equipment | Term dates and cancellation notice |
| Care or household help | Availability, booked shifts, capabilities, assigned duties | Extra-hours lead time, task-list deadline, backup care |
| Food | Preferences, restrictions, desired planning support | Prep opportunities, shopping, weekly rotation, travel storage |
| Travel | Expected trips, itinerary source, planning lead times | Home coverage, meals, transport, packing, return handoff |
| Tools | Products, accounts, workspaces, purpose, source of truth | Access scope, unavailable accounts, manual fallback |
| Planning loop | Preferred interview time, brief format, morning check, review | Optional reminder after the user requests one |
| Exercise | Desired activities, duration, equipment, feasible windows | Existing constraints the user wants considered |

## Question examples

- Work: "Which days do you work on-site, and when do you need to arrive and leave?"
- Commute: "How long is the trip in each direction, including the usual delay?"
- Care: "Who normally handles that pickup, and is that confirmed for the coming week?"
- Alternation: "Which dated weekend is the anchor, and when does the handoff begin and end?"
- Tools: "Which apps hold your calendar, tasks, work projects, and messages?"
- Access: "Which accounts and projects may I check, and which should stay manual?"
- Priorities: "What result would make the next month successful?"
- Readiness: "The weekday pattern is covered; should meals stay deferred while we plan the first day?"

A response may answer several areas at once.
Record the answered areas and choose the next question from the remaining gaps.
Do not ask about children's schools when dependent care is not applicable.
Do not collect health diagnoses, account credentials, or exact locations merely to fill a template.
